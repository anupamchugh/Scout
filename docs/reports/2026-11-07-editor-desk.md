## Method and limits

- gitconnected is a single publication feed: its latest ~10 posts, often from one day. It is not a trend.
- Coffee Bytes writers are the 148 Medium handles linked from the owner's 31 Coffee Bytes issues (Jun 2022 onward), read through their own feeds. Most no longer post; only active writers are judged here.
- gitconnected writers mostly have no Medium handle in their links (custom domain), so their trajectories are not available.
- Member-only posts carry a preview in feeds; those are marked preview only, not scored.
- Long articles are judged on their opening, trimmed to fit the on-device context window.
- No story was fetched from a web page; Medium pages refuse automated reads, so only RSS text was used.
- The two groups differ in time, topic and selection; differences in averages describe these samples only.
